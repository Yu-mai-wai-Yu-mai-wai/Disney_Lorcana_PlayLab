## [The goal](https://lorcana.gg/rules/#the-goal)

Lorcana is a race, not a brawl: send characters on quests to gather lore, and the first player to 20 lore wins on the spot.

You gain **lore** mainly by **questing** — exerting a character to collect its lore value — and from locations, which pay out automatically each turn. Combat exists, but it never touches your opponent directly: characters challenge characters to knock questers off the table. Whoever reaches **20 lore** first wins immediately, even in the middle of a turn.

Three **Golden Rules** sit above everything else here: card text beats the rulebook when they disagree; an effect that **prevents** something beats an effect that allows it; and when an effect can only partly happen, you do as much as you can rather than nothing.

Common mistake

There is a second way the game ends, and it is not the one most card games teach you: a player who ends their turn with an empty deck LOSES. Failing to draw from an empty deck is, by itself, harmless — the axe only falls when your own turn ends. Details in Winning & losing.

## [Building a deck](https://lorcana.gg/rules/#building-a-deck)

60 cards minimum, at most 2 of the 6 ink types, and no more than 4 copies of any full name.

|Rule|Requirement|
|---|---|
|Deck size|At least **60 cards**. No maximum — but every extra card dilutes your best draws.|
|Ink types|At most **2** of the six inks: Amber, Amethyst, Emerald, Ruby, Sapphire, Steel. A dual-ink card counts as both of its types.|
|Copies|Up to **4 cards with the same full name** — name plus version. [Elsa - Snow Queen](https://lorcana.gg/cards/P1-003-elsa-snow-queen) and [Elsa - Spirit of Winter](https://lorcana.gg/cards/001-042-elsa-spirit-of-winter) are different full names, so 4 of each is legal.|
|Banned cards|None allowed in Constructed. The current list is at the bottom of this page — it is two cards.|

Common mistake

The 4-copy limit reads the FULL name, but card effects that look for a name read only the name line. To an effect that says "your characters named Elsa", every version of Elsa is simply "Elsa" — which is also exactly how Shift finds its target.

## [Starting a game](https://lorcana.gg/rules/#starting-a-game)

Draw 7, alter your hand once if you want — and remember the starting player skips their first draw.

1First player2Shuffle3Draw 74Alter your hand5Begin

First player

Decide the starting player randomly — dice, coin, whatever the table agrees on. In a series after game one, the loser of the previous game chooses whether to go first.

Common mistake

The starting player skips the Draw step of the first turn — that single card is the entire compensation for going first. Tables that forget it hand the player on the play a free card every game.

## [The zones](https://lorcana.gg/rules/#zones)

Five physical zones per player — deck, hand, play, inkwell, discard — plus the bag, which exists only in the rules.

- **Deck** — facedown, order secret, no peeking or rearranging. Anyone may count any deck at any time.
- **Hand** — private, and there is **no maximum hand size**. Only the count is public.
- **Play** — one zone holding your characters, items, and locations together. Public. You cannot play cards into an opponent's play zone.
- **Inkwell** — your facedown resources. Private even to you: once a card is ink, nobody looks at its face again, ever.
- **Discard** — faceup, public, and you may rearrange your own freely.
- **The bag** — not a physical place: the holding area where triggered abilities wait to resolve. Covered under Abilities & the bag.

Good to know

Leaving play is a hard reset: damage counters, gained keywords, and every other effect on a card cease to exist the moment it changes zones. A character bounced to hand comes back as a stranger.

## [Card types](https://lorcana.gg/rules/#card-types)

Characters quest and fight, actions resolve once, songs can be sung, items and locations stay and work immediately.

|Type|What it does|
|---|---|
|Character|Your cast. Has Strength (damage it deals), Willpower (damage it takes before being banished), and a lore value (what questing earns). Enters play **ready but drying** — it cannot act until your next turn.|
|Action|A one-shot effect: pay, resolve immediately, straight to the discard. Actions never wait in play and their effects never enter the bag.|
|Action — Song|An action with a bonus way to pay: instead of ink, **exert one character with ink cost equal to or greater than the song's cost**. Details under Singing songs.|
|Item|A tool that stays in play. Items enter ready and — unlike characters — **can use their abilities the turn they arrive**.|
|Location|A landscape card that sits in play, holds your characters (move them there for its move cost), and pays out its lore automatically every one of your turns. Never ready nor exerted; challengeable at any time.|

Common mistake

The action asymmetry: items and locations work immediately, characters do not. A character enters play ready — nothing to do with being exerted — but "drying", and drying shuts off questing, challenging, and exerting to pay for anything until the start of your next turn.

## [Ink & the inkwell](https://lorcana.gg/rules/#ink-and-inkwell)

Any card with the inkwell symbol can become your resource — once per turn, facedown, and spendable immediately.

There are no resource cards in Lorcana. Instead, **once during your turn** you may take a card with the **inkwell symbol** (the swirl around its cost) from your hand, reveal it, and put it into your inkwell **facedown and ready**. Every card in your inkwell is worth exactly 1 ink — what is printed on its face stops mattering forever. To pay a cost, exert that many ready ink cards.

Good to know

Ink enters ready, so the card you ink this turn pays for this turn's plays — ink first, then spend. The real decision is what you can bear to lose: inking is effectively permanent removal of that card from your game.

Common mistake

Only cards showing the inkwell symbol may be inked with the turn action — roughly the format's stronger cards leave it off on purpose. If your hand is all uninkable bombs, you are not building an inkwell that turn.

## [The turn](https://lorcana.gg/rules/#the-turn)

Ready, Set, Draw — then a Main Phase where you act in any order, and nothing at all happens on your opponent's turn.

1Ready2Set3Draw4Main Phase5End of turn

Ready

Ready every card you have in play and in your inkwell. "During your turn" effects switch on; "until the start of your next turn" effects end.

Good to know

There is no instant speed in Lorcana. Only the active player plays cards and uses activated abilities, and nothing can be played in response to anything — your only interaction on an opponent's turn is triggered abilities firing on their own. Plan on your turn; watch on theirs.

Common mistake

Drying ends at the SET step, not "when the turn starts" and not when the card readies. The sequence on your turn is: ready everything, THEN characters become dry. A character that somehow enters play during your own turn is drying and stays drying until your next Set step.

## [Questing](https://lorcana.gg/rules/#questing)

Exert a dry character, gain its lore value. It is how you win — and it is also how you become attackable.

To **quest**, pick one of your **dry** characters, exert it, and gain lore equal to its lore value. That is the whole engine of the game — [Mickey Mouse - Brave Little Tailor](https://lorcana.gg/cards/D23-001-mickey-mouse-brave-little-tailor) questing for 4 is a fifth of your win in one action. But an exerted character is exactly what your opponent is allowed to challenge, so every quest is also an invitation.

That tension — **quest and be exposed, or hold ready and stay safe** — is the central decision of every Lorcana turn. A character standing ready cannot be challenged at all; the moment it quests, it is a target until your next Ready step.

Common mistake

A character played this turn is drying and cannot quest, full stop. Rush waives the challenge restriction only — NOTHING lets a character quest the turn it comes down. If your plan needs lore this turn, it needs characters that are already dry.

## [Challenges](https://lorcana.gg/rules/#challenges)

Your character attacks an EXERTED opposing character — never a ready one — and both hit each other at once.

1Declare2Pick the target3Check restrictions4Damage5Aftermath

Declare

Choose one of your characters to challenge. It must be **dry and ready** — in play since the beginning of your Set step (or have Rush).

Common mistake

You can only challenge an EXERTED character. This is the single most misplayed rule in Lorcana: there is no attacking a ready blocker, no forcing a fight with a fresh character, and a drying character cannot be challenged either — it is ready, just unusable. Characters only become vulnerable when they exert: questing, singing, or paying costs.

Common mistake

Damage does not heal. Not at end of turn, not at the start of yours — it accumulates until the card is banished, an effect like [Hakuna Matata](https://lorcana.gg/cards/001-027-hakuna-matata) removes it, or the card leaves play. Two 3-damage challenges kill a 6-Willpower character just as surely as one big hit.

Example

Your opponent's [Cheshire Cat - Not All There](https://lorcana.gg/cards/001-071-cheshire-cat-not-all-there) (0 Strength, 3 Willpower) sits exerted after questing. You challenge with a 3-Strength, 3-Willpower character: the Cat takes 3 and is banished, your character takes 0 — but the Cat's ability banishes the challenger right back. Reading the exerted quester before swinging is the skill.

## [Singing songs](https://lorcana.gg/rules/#singing-songs)

Songs are actions your characters can pay for: exert one character whose ink cost meets the song's cost, and it is free.

Every **song** carries the same built-in deal: instead of paying ink, you may **exert one of your characters with ink cost equal to or greater than the song's cost**. The character must be dry — singing is exerting to pay a cost, and drying characters cannot do that. The song then resolves exactly as if you had paid ink for it.

Two keywords widen the deal. **Singer N** lets a character sing as though its cost were N: [Ariel - Spectacular Singer](https://lorcana.gg/cards/cc1-001-ariel-spectacular-singer) costs 3 ink but sings like a 5-cost, covering [Hakuna Matata](https://lorcana.gg/cards/001-027-hakuna-matata) with room to spare. **Sing Together N** lets several characters pool their ink costs to hit N — how a table of cheap characters belts out [Be Prepared](https://lorcana.gg/cards/001-128-be-prepared).

Good to know

Singing exerts the character, which makes it challengeable — but so would questing, and the song was free. Also worth knowing: Reckless characters can sing. They can't quest, but nothing stops them from carrying a tune before they brawl.

## [Abilities & the bag](https://lorcana.gg/rules/#abilities-and-the-bag)

Four shapes of ability, and one queue — the bag — where triggered abilities wait their turn.

|Shape|How to spot it|How it works|
|---|---|---|
|Triggered|"When…", "Whenever…", "At the start/end of…"|Fires itself when its condition happens and goes to the **bag** to resolve. The only kind of ability that uses the bag.|
|Activated|"_cost_ — _effect_", often with the exert symbol|Pay the cost, resolve the effect immediately. Active player only, Main Phase only.|
|Static|A statement of fact: "Your characters get +1 Strength."|Continuously on — either while its card is in play or for a printed duration. Applies instantly, no bag.|
|Replacement|"…instead"|Rewrites an event before it happens. The original event never occurred, so nothing triggers off it.|

The **bag** is Lorcana's resolution queue: when triggers pile up, the **active player resolves all of theirs first, one at a time, in any order they choose** — then the bag passes to the next player for theirs. New triggers created along the way just join the bag and wait.

Common mistake

An exert symbol in an activated ability's cost needs a dry character — the same drying rule as questing. But an ability WITHOUT an exert cost works even on a drying or exerted character, and items and locations never dry at all.

Example

[Beast - Selfless Protector](https://lorcana.gg/cards/002-172-beast-selfless-protector) reads "Whenever one of your other characters would be dealt damage, put that many damage counters on this character instead." That "instead" is a replacement: the protected character was never dealt damage, so nothing that triggers on it taking damage fires — the event happened only to Beast.

|Keyword|What it means|
|---|---|

|**Bodyguard**|May enter play exerted; while it stands, opponents challenging your characters must pick a Bodyguard if they can. [Maximus - Palace Horse](https://lorcana.gg/cards/001-010-maximus-palace-horse) is the classic wall.|
|**Boost N**|Once per your turn, pay N ink to tuck your deck's top card facedown under this card.|
|**Challenger +N**|+N Strength **while challenging**. Stacks.|
|**Evasive**|Can be challenged only by characters with Evasive (or Alert).|
|**Reckless**|Can't quest — and you can't end your turn while it stands ready with a legal challenge available.|
|**Resist +N**|Damage dealt to it is reduced by N. Stacks. Doesn't touch damage that is "put" or moved.|
|**Rush**|May challenge the turn it comes into play. Challenging only — it still can't quest or exert to pay.|
|**Shift N**|Alternate cost: pay N to play this on top of your character with the same name, inheriting its state. Variants exist for classifications, (Universal Shift) any character, and the Duo, Combo, Temporary, and Potato forms.|
|**Singer N**|Sings songs as though its ink cost were N.|
|**Sing Together N**|Exert any number of your characters with total ink cost N or more to sing this song.|
|**Support**|Whenever it quests, you may add its Strength to another chosen character's Strength this turn.|
|**Vanish**|Banished when an opponent chooses it while resolving an action.|
|**Ward**|Opponents can't **choose** it with effects. Challenges and no-choice effects still hit it.|

Common mistake

Challenger +N works exactly one way: it applies while this character is the challenger and never while it is being challenged. A Challenger +3 with 1 Strength hits for 4 on your turn and still defends at 1 on your opponent's.

Example

The new Elsa inherits the old one's states — she comes down DRY and can quest or challenge immediately. She also inherits any damage, and when she leaves play the whole stack goes with her.

## [Winning & losing](https://lorcana.gg/rules/#winning-and-losing)

20 lore wins instantly. An empty deck loses — but only when your own turn ends.

- **20 or more lore** — you win the moment a game state check sees it, even mid-turn, even on an opponent's turn.
- **Your turn ends with zero cards in your deck** — you lose at that end-of-turn check.
- **Last player standing** in a multiplayer game wins.
- **Concession** — allowed at any time, immediate loss.

Common mistake

Running out of deck is not the loss — ending YOUR turn with an empty deck is. Failing to draw does nothing by itself, so a player milled to zero on an opponent's turn gets one full turn to reach 20 lore before their own end-of-turn check finishes them. Milling as a strategy has to survive that last swing.

Ravensburger has banned exactly two cards in the game's history: Hiram Flaversham - Toymaker (Core and Infinity Constructed) and Fortisphere (Core Constructed). There is no restricted list — the publisher's usual lever is errata, not bans.

Both bans hit degenerate item-loop draw engines: [Hiram Flaversham - Toymaker](https://lorcana.gg/cards/002-149-hiram-flaversham-toymaker) and [Fortisphere](https://lorcana.gg/cards/004-200-fortisphere) were banned together from **Core Constructed** on **April 8, 2025**. Hiram was later banned from **Infinity Constructed** as well, effective **March 26, 2026**.

The publisher's preferred tool for power outliers is **errata** rather than bans: [Bucky - Squirrel Squeak Tutor](https://lorcana.gg/cards/P1-015-bucky-squirrel-squeak-tutor) was rewritten effective August 9, 2024 — cost raised from 2 to 3, Ward removed, and its discard trigger narrowed to Floodborn shifts. The card stays legal, playing its current official text regardless of what an older printing says.

Good to know

Bans bind sanctioned play — Disney Lorcana Challenge events and tournaments in the named formats. Casual tables are explicitly free to keep playing these cards, and Ravensburger says so in the ban announcements themselves.
